# RigFlow v0.2.0 --- Maya 2026.3 validation record

Target environment: **Autodesk Maya 2026.3 / Windows**.

This document updates the original pre-release checklist after the
manual v0.2.0 QA session. It deliberately distinguishes **verified**,
**partially verified**, and **not verified in this session** items
rather than marking unobserved requirements as passed.

## Status legend

-   **\[x\] Verified** --- directly observed during the completed Maya
    QA session.
-   **\[\~\] Partial** --- relevant behavior was exercised, but not
    every original sub-check was independently verified.
-   **\[ \] Not verified** --- no sufficient evidence was captured in
    this session.

## QA conclusion

**Core tested v0.2.0 workflows: PASS.**

The completed Maya 2026.3 session provides direct evidence for the
primary artist-facing validation, audit, safe-fix,
preflight/export-gating, batch-export, post-export integrity and
OpenMaya scanner workflows.

The original checklist's strict acceptance gate required every mandatory
item A--H to pass. Because several sub-tests (notably Validator Registry
extension/error injection, explicit scanner fallback comparison, some
report/preset assertions and clip sanitization) were **not independently
verified in the captured session**, this record does **not** silently
mark those requirements as passed.

For portfolio/release wording, v0.2.0 may accurately be described as
**manually QA-tested in Maya 2026.3 for the documented core workflows,
with remaining unverified checklist items explicitly recorded above**.
Unity/Unreal engine-side import is also outside the verified scope.
